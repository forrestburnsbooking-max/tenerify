## Jet ski from Los Gigantes
- **Jet Ski Safari Los Gigantes** (via Club Canary; base operator at Los Gigantes harbour) — ~1h on water, **direct start from Puerto de Los Gigantes** (no transfer). Single €130 (€30 deposit, €100 on day) / Double €140 (€35 deposit, €105 on day). Includes guide, life vest + wetsuit, briefing, showers/towels/lockers, insurance. Drive 16+ (parental consent), passenger 8+, driver 18+, under-24 bring ID.
- Note: most other "Los Gigantes" jet ski safaris actually depart from **Puerto Colón** (JetScoot, Tenerife World of Watersports ~3h single €180/double €220) or **Las Galletas** (JetSki Holiday). Our catalogue jetski group already covers Puerto Colón.
